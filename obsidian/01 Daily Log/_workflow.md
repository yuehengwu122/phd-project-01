## Daily Log Workflow

This folder is for lightweight weekly planning and review.

### Goal
Use one weekly markdown file to track:
- weekly todo
- daily todo
- pomodoro sessions
- short daily review
- weekly review

Keep it simple. Do not turn this into a heavy task system.

### How Codex should help

1. Morning dump
When I send a brain dump of tasks, help me clean it up into a short `Todo` list for that day.
Keep my wording when possible.
Do not over-structure.
If needed, also help me update the top-level `Week Todo`.

2. Start pomo
When I say `start pomo`, help me define one concrete `Plan` for the next 50-minute session.

3. End pomo
When I say `end pomo`, help me fill in:
- `Done`
- `Note`

`Note` can include blockers, distraction, feelings, or next-step reminders.

4. Daily review
When I say `daily review`, summarize the day under `What did I do today.` in short bullets.

5. Weekly review
When I say `weekly review`, help me summarize:
- what moved forward
- what got stuck
- repeated distractions
- one adjustment for next week

### Style
- Keep it short and usable.
- Prefer simple bullets.
- Avoid building a complex productivity system.
- Do not rewrite personal reflections unless asked.
